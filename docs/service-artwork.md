# Service artwork

The service icons identify third-party services. They were downloaded from the artwork in each service's Apple App Store listing: Pagebound's on 2026-10-09, the others on 2026-10-06. The bundled 512 × 512 JPEG files are unchanged downloads, not generated or recoloured icons. Android scales them to fit the interface; there is no runtime download.

These brand marks belong to their respective owners. The project's MIT licence does not grant rights to third-party artwork or trademarks. Their presence does not imply endorsement.
